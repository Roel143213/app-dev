## About the movie

**Drawing Closer** is an *emotional Japanese romantic drama film* on Netflix that centers on *terminal illness*,*art*, and *finding purpose in the face of death*. The story follows ***Akito Hayasaka*** a talented *17-year-old artist* whose life is turned upside down when he is *diagnosed with a serious heart tumor* and given only one year left to live. Overwhelmed by *fear*, he gives up on painting and contemplates ending his life on a hospital rooftop. There, he meets ***Haruna Sakurai***, a cheerful girl who is also terminally ill with only six months left to live.

Unlike ***Akito***, ***Haruna*** accepts her fate with grace, sketching angels and skies to represent *freedom*. Inspired by her *resilience*, ***Akito*** begins visiting her regularly, and their shared pain soon blossoms into romance.***Haruna*** encourages ***Akito*** to pick up his pencils again, helping him realize that drawing is a way to fight despair and give meaning to his remaining time. 

While ***Akito*** initially hides his diagnosis to protect her, a heartbreaking twist reveals that Haruna knew about his condition all along, choosing to love him fiercely in silence. As their time runs out, ***Haruna*** *passes away*, leaving ***Akito*** grief-stricken. Ultimately, *he finds the strength to honor her memory by completing his abandoned art exhibition*, with his *final masterpiece* being a glowing depiction of *Heaven*. Closing on a hopeful note, the film shows ***Akito*** choosing to live out his final days with purpose, **proving that love and creation can outlast even death.**
